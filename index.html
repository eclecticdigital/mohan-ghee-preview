बिलोना · The Wooden Churn
4 hrsOf hand-churning
The Bilona Method

Four hours of churning.
Twenty-seven of patience.

The bilona is a wooden churn older than memory. We do not modernise it. The cream is cultured into curd, set overnight, and turned by hand until the butter rises like sunlight to the top.

01

The Cow

Indigenous Gir, Sahiwal & Murrah. Free-grazed. Milk only after the calf has had her share.

02

The Curd

Whole milk, cultured with yesterday's curd. Set in clay pots, overnight, in the cool of dawn.

03

The Churn

Two women, a wooden bilona, four hours of rhythm. Butter rises. Buttermilk falls.

04

The Flame

Slow-simmered in copper kadhai over wood-fire until white butter turns gold.

Three Generations · One Recipe

Begun in 1947.
Carried in a thousand kitchens.

What started as a clay pot of ghee on a winter morning in 1947 — the year of India's freedom — became a small, stubborn promise. We make ghee the way our grandmother taught us. And on most days, we still make it the way she watches us.

1947

Mohan Ghee Products is founded in the year of independence. The first yellow tin leaves the kitchen.

1980s

A Household Name

Three generations across North India grow up on the same recipe. The trade-mark, the iron tin, unchanged.

2010s

The Bilona Revival

The wooden bilona returns to the centre of our kitchen, even as the rest of the industry mechanises.
