The Bilona Method

The Bilona Journey

Four generations of tradition in every spoonful

01

The A2 Advantage

We source pure A2 milk from indigenous Gir cows and Murrah buffaloes, grazing freely in natural pastures. A2 beta-casein protein is easier to digest and closer to what your grandmother used.

02

Setting the Curd

We don't use cream. We use whole milk, set into curd overnight in earthen matkas, just like Dadi used to. The clay pots keep it cool naturally and add minerals — the foundation of true bilona.

03

The Bilona Churn

Before dawn, we churn the curd using a traditional wooden bilona — clockwise and anti-clockwise — to extract the purest Makhan (butter). This slow process preserves nutrients that machines destroy.

04

Slow Firewood Heating

The Makhan is heated on low firewood flames for hours. This slow heating locks in the rich danedar (granular) texture, deep aroma, and golden colour that defines authentic Bilona ghee.
